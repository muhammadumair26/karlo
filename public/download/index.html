⬇  Install Karlo for Android
Test version 1.0 · about 6 MB · Android 7 or newer
  1. Tap Install Karlo for Android and open the download.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (this is normal for apps not from the Play Store).
  3. Tap Install, open Karlo and sign in with your Karlo email and password.
📱 On an iPhone? The iPhone app isn't available for download yet — use karlo.orionsoftsol.com in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
